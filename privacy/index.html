How Bootstrap handles your information.

Last updated: April 28, 2026

Overview

Bootstrap LLC ("Bootstrap," "we," "us," or "our") operates the Bootstrap desktop application and the website at tldr.is. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our services or communicate with us.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: email address and password when you create an account.
  • Usage data: information about how you use the app, including file processing activity, to improve the service.
  • Communications: messages you send us via email or SMS.
  • Phone number: if you opt into SMS messaging, we collect the mobile number you provide.

SMS Messaging

If you opt into Bootstrap SMS updates, we will use your phone number solely to send messages related to the Bootstrap messaging program — including product updates, download links, and support responses. We do not sell or share your phone number with third parties for their marketing purposes.

Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe. Reply HELP for assistance or contact us at hello@tldr.is.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the Bootstrap app and services.
  • To respond to your inquiries and support requests.
  • To send transactional and product-related communications you have opted into.
  • To improve and develop Bootstrap features.

Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers who assist in operating our services (such as cloud infrastructure and AI processing), subject to confidentiality obligations. We may disclose information if required by law.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by emailing hello@tldr.is.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at hello@tldr.is.
